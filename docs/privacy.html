Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Pickleball Team Flow respects your privacy. The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to Pickleball Team Flow.

Player Names

Player names entered into the app are used to create pickleball court assignments, player rotations, and matchups. Pickleball Team Flow does not upload player names to a Pickleball Team Flow server.

Photos and Camera Access

The app may use the device camera or photo library to help recognize player names from an image. Images selected for name recognition are processed for that purpose and are not stored by Pickleball Team Flow.

Accounts

Pickleball Team Flow does not currently require or provide user accounts.

Data Sharing

Pickleball Team Flow does not sell personal information or player information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
